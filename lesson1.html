What is a Game?

A game is a situation where the outcome depends on choices made by more than one person or firm. It's not just about you—others' decisions matter too.

Key Terms

Player – a person or firm making a choice.
Strategy – one possible action.
Payoff – the result or reward from a choice.

Strategic Thinking

Players must think about what others might do. Your choice should respond to their likely actions.

Two Types of Games

Simultaneous game – players choose at the same time.
Sequential game – one player chooses first.

Your best choice depends on the other player.
Two Coffee Shops
Two coffee shops on the same street each choose High Price or Low Price. The numbers show daily profits.
Shop 2: High Price Shop 2: Low Price
Shop 1: High Price 4, 4 1, 5
Shop 1: Low Price 5, 1 2, 2

If both choose High Price, both do well.

If one chooses Low Price, that shop gets more customers.

The choice of one shop changes the result for the other.
