Dominant Strategy

Dominant strategy – a strategy that gives a better result than another choice.

P1
A B
X 5, 3 4, 2
Y 2, 4 3, 1

X is better than Y in both columns.

Strict Dominance

Strict dominance – one strategy is better in every case.

P2
P1
A B
X 5, 3 4, 2
Y 2, 4 3, 1

X gives higher payoff in every column.

Weak Dominance

Weak dominance – one strategy is never worse and sometimes better.

P2
P1
A B
X 3, 3 4, 2
Y 3, 4 2, 1

X is equal in one column, better in the other.

Dominated Strategy

Dominated strategy – a strategy that should be removed.

P1

IEDS: Iterated Elimination of Dominated Strategies
Remove dominated strategies step by step.
P2
P1
A B C
X 4, 2 3, 3 2, 4
Y 1, 1 2, 2 1, 3
Z 3, 1 4, 2 1, 3

Step 1: Pick best responses in full matrix.

Step 2: Row Y is eliminated. Pick best responses now (X/Z vs A/B/C).

Step 3: Column A is eliminated. Pick best responses now (X/Z vs B/C).

Step 4: Column B is eliminated.

Step 5: Pick best responses with rows X/Z and columns B/C.

Step 6: Eliminate column B (3rd elimination).

Step 7: Pick best response in remaining column C.

Step 8: Eliminate row Z (4th elimination).

Final IEDS solution: (X, C) | payoff (2, 4).
